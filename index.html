Hardware development shouldn't require managing ten different vendors.

A typical electronics project touches a design house, a distributor, a fab, an assembler, a test lab and a logistics partner — each with separate quotes, timelines and blind spots. When something slips, everyone points elsewhere.

THE BEVARON POSITION

Bevaron Systems is the single coordinated partner across that whole chain. We own the engineering, sourcing, quality and delivery — so you work with one capable hardware company, not a crowd of vendors.

LEGACY ELECTRONICS

When the original board is gone, the product doesn't have to be.

Machines outlive their electronics. When a controller is discontinued, its parts are obsolete and no documentation survives, we reconstruct what's needed to keep the product running — engineering-led, and only where our customers are legally authorized to reproduce the design.

  • Obsolescence recovery — source remaining life, map EOL parts, plan the bridge.
  • Redesign — re-engineer the board with current components, same function and footprint.
  • Reproduction — rebuild and test replacement boards, delivered production-ready.

Authorized work only. Reverse engineering and reproduction are undertaken only where the customer holds the rights or written authorization to the design. We verify before we begin.

ILLUSTRATIVE PROJECT

Legacy controller redesign

A discontinued industrial controller, EOL across multiple parts and no surviving design files. We recovered the schematic, re-engineered the board with current components and reproduced a verified replacement batch.

  • Schematic recovered via board reverse-engineering, with full IP authorization
  • Every EOL part mapped to a verified current equivalent
  • Small batch delivered with test records and documentation package
ILLUSTRATIVE PROJECT

New product prototype build

A connected-device startup with a working breadboard concept. We produced the production-intent schematic, PCB layout and enclosure integration, then prototyped and debugged through two board revisions.

  • Two prototype iterations, each with a documented change list
  • BOM optimized for availability before the pilot build
  • Design package ready for pilot production handoff
